# A clearer home for Hark and its insights

Status: Approved
Approved by: Product owner, in the September 30, 2026 chat: “Looks good. Go ahead and build it out, please.”

## Problem

“I'd like to do another pass on updating the UI and giving it a new modern feel for Hark. Please take inspiration from how Wispr flow does it. I'd like our stats screens to have more stats, kind of like the screenshot I sent from the insights page as well. Show me how you would update the UI. I'd like to have a dark and a light mode as well. And a solarized dark and solarized light mode.”

## Desired outcome

A calmer, modern interface with a welcoming Home screen, clear navigation, richer useful insights into personal dictation, and four coherent appearances. Preserve Hark's existing capabilities and local-first behavior.

## Accepted direction

The owner approved the interactive preview showing Home, Insights (Overview, Your voice, Performance), History, Meetings, Spellbook, Invocations, and Appearance. Sample numbers illustrate layout only. The approval authorizes implementing the design with real local data; it does not authorize fabricated metrics or uploading history for analysis.

## Lessons Learned / Gotchas

Historical clip duration and correction/app metadata are not recoverable from existing history. Existing history retention bounds what can be reconstructed. Missing measurements must stay unknown.
