# Intent: control meetings from the keyboard

Status: Approved scope

This delivery covers item 3 of the approved [Meetings plan](../../tasks/2026-09-26-plan-meeting-transcription.md#polish). The product owner authorized commit, push, and tag operations; the parent task owns them.
